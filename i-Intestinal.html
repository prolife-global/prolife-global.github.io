Probiotics → Motility, Stool Consistency & IBS Symptoms

  • Bifidobacterium strains (e.g., B. lactis HN019) shorten whole-gut transit time and ease functional constipation in adults (randomized trials & meta-analyses).[1,2]
  • Multi-strain probiotics modestly reduce global IBS symptoms, abdominal pain, and bloating in systematic reviews.[3]

Prebiotic Fiber → SCFAs, Mucus Layer & Motility

  • Fermentable/viscous fibers feed beneficial microbes to produce short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) like butyrate that strengthen barrier function, regulate motility, and calm mucosal inflammation.[4]

Vitamin D3 → Barrier Integrity & Immune Balance

  • Vitamin D receptors on epithelial/immune cells help maintain tight-junction integrity and balanced mucosal immunity that’s critical for gut comfort.[5]

Vitamin K2 (MK-7) → Normal Coagulation & Tissue Repair

  • Vitamin K contributes to normal blood clotting, supporting healthy tissue repair processes in the GI tract; MK-7 is a well-absorbed, long-acting form.[6]
