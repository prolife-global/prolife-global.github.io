Positive Reactions

When starting probiotic nutritional drinks, some short-term, mild reactions can appear. These are usually signs of adjustment, detox and re-balancing — not harmful side effects.

開始飲用益生菌營養液時，出現的短暫輕微反應多屬身體調整與排毒訊號，並非副作用。

Common Positive Reactions （常見好轉反應）
Digestive shift

Gas • mild diarrhea • appetite change
脹氣、輕微腹瀉、食慾改變

Detox signs

Thirst • frequent urination
口渴、尿多

Skin flare

Temporary itch/pimples
短暫痕癢／暗瘡

Energy & mood

Tired first → then steadier
初期較疲倦 → 其後更穩定

Typical timeline / 常見時間線
Days 1–3

Digestive bubbles, thirst, light fatigue.
腸胃蠕動增加、口渴、輕微疲倦。

Week 1–2

Bowel pattern stabilises; skin may purge slightly.
排便漸穩；皮膚可能短暫排毒。

Week 3–4

Energy & sleep improve for most people.
多數人精神與睡眠改善。

Details by system / 分系統詳情

  • Gas / loose stool / appetite shift 脹氣、便較稀、食慾改變
  • Stomach discomfort may appear then ease 胃口不適，後漸改善
  • Ulcer/hemorrhoid areas can feel tender short-term 潰瘍／痔瘡位置短暫不適

  • Temporary itch, acne may worsen then clear 痕癢／暗瘡初期加劇後回落
  • Rashes possible with liver/gut detox 排毒期間或出疹

  • Values can fluctuate while re-balancing 調整期數值有波動
  • Diabetes: tingling, fatigue may appear 糖尿：手腳麻、疲倦
  • Hypertension: brief heaviness or dizziness 高血壓：短暫沉重感／頭暈

  • Menstrual flow or timing may adjust 經期量與時間或調整
  • Cramps/clots for a few days can happen 短暫經痛／經塊

  • More phlegm / thicker nasal discharge 痰增、多且較黏稠
  • Temporary dizziness/nausea in bronchitis 支氣管炎可一度頭暈想吐

  • Pain might increase before easing 痛楚先升後回
  • Rheumatism/gout may feel sore for days 風濕／痛風數天酸痛

  • Light insomnia / vivid dreams 輕度失眠／多夢
  • Temporary irritability / low mood 短暫煩躁／情緒低落
What to do / 如何應對
Keep going 繼續飲用
  • Mild digestive changes, thirst, light fatigue
    輕微腸胃變化、口渴、疲倦
  • Skin flare that eases within ~1–2 weeks
    皮膚反應約 1–2 週內減退
Slow down 放慢或減量
  • Discomfort affecting sleep/work
    不適影響睡眠或工作
  • Sensitive groups (pregnant, chronic disease) adjust with guidance
    懷孕／慢病者宜按指引調整
Seek medical advice 盡快求醫
  • Severe pain, persistent bleeding, high fever
    劇痛、持續出血、高燒
  • Symptoms > 2–3 weeks or getting worse
    超過 2–3 週未改善或加劇
Summary 摘要

Short-term reactions usually settle within days to weeks and often signal re-balancing. If symptoms persist, consult a healthcare professional.
大多於數天至數週內消退，多屬好轉現象；如持續不適，請諮詢醫護人員。
