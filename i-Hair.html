Biotin + Zinc: Keratin & Root Strength

  • Biotin (3 mg) acts as a cofactor in keratin infrastructure and hair-shaft integrity; deficiency is linked with hair thinning.
  • Zinc (15 mg) supports follicular matrix cell proliferation, sebum balance, and immune defense around the follicle.

Sources: Patel et al., 2017, Skin Appendage Disord. (Biotin & hair) · Almohanna et al., 2019, Dermatol Ther (Heidelb). (Vitamins/minerals & hair)

Vitamin D3 + K2: Follicle Cycling & Micro-circulation

  • Vitamin D3 (20 µg / 800 IU) supports hair-follicle cycling through vitamin-D–receptor signaling, associated with healthy anagen maintenance.
  • Vitamin K2 (MK-7, 30 µg) complements D3 and contributes to vascular elasticity and calcium handling, supporting nutrient delivery to scalp tissues.

Sources: Cianferotti & Baldini, 2012, Br J Dermatol. (Vitamin D & hair biology) · K2–vascular elasticity overview

Probiotics: Calm Scalp Inflammation & Barrier

  • Selected Lactobacillus / Bifidobacterium strains can modulate systemic inflammation (IL-6/TNF-α), promote regulatory T-cell tone, and enhance epithelial barrier factors that influence scalp comfort and hair retention.
  • Microbial metabolites (e.g., SCFAs) may support hair shaft quality and reduce oxidative stress load to follicles.

Sources: O’Neill et al., 2020, npj Biofilms & Microbiomes. (Gut–immune axis) · Levkovich et al., 2013, PLoS ONE. (L. reuteri, skin/hair glow in vivo)
