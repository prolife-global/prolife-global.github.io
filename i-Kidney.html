i-Kidney® 益腎寶 — Renal Strength & Circulatory Balance

i-Kidney® 益腎寶 is a Probiotic Nutritional Drink (益生營養液飲料) formulated to support kidney function and systemic harmony. A scientifically informed blend of probiotics and micronutrients helps maintain healthy filtration, fluid balance, and vascular resilience.

Why It Works: Mechanism of Action

i-Kidney® aligns the gut–kidney–vascular axis. Specific probiotics and prebiotic fiber help reduce gut-derived uremic toxins, while postbiotic SCFAs and carefully chosen micronutrients support barrier integrity, immune balance, and circulatory homeostasis.

Proudly Made in Hong Kong — Gut-Driven Renal Support

Gut–Kidney Axis & Postbiotics

  • CKD is associated with gut dysbiosis and higher gut-derived uremic toxins (e.g., p-cresyl sulfate, indoxyl sulfate).[1]
  • Prebiotic fiber and synbiotic strategies can lower p-cresyl sulfate/indolic toxins in clinical trials and meta-analyses.[2][3]
  • SCFAs (e.g., acetate/propionate) signal via renal GPCRs (Olfr78/GPR41) that contribute to blood-pressure and renin regulation.[4][5]

Barrier Integrity & Immune Tone

  • Urea and CKD-related factors can disrupt tight-junction proteins and increase intestinal permeability (“leaky gut”).[6]
  • Probiotics/prebiotics are associated with reduced systemic inflammation (e.g., lower CRP) in renal populations.[3]

Circulatory Balance

  • SCFA signaling has been shown to modulate vascular tone and attenuate hypertensive damage in experimental models.[5]
  • Supports comfortable fluid balance and vascular resilience as part of a balanced lifestyle.

Micronutrient Support

  • Vitamin D has broad immunomodulatory roles that help keep immune signaling appropriately regulated.[7]
  • Vitamin K (e.g., MK-7) improves vitamin-K–dependent protein status (dp-ucMGP) in HD patients; effects on clinical calcification outcomes remain under study.[8][9]
