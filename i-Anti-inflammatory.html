i-Anti-Inflammatory® 抗炎寶 – Immunity & Inflammation Balance

i-Anti-Inflammator® is a Probiotic Nutritional Drink (益生營養液飲料) designed to address systemic inflammation and promote immune equilibrium. It combines targeted probiotics with anti-inflammatory nutrients to help modulate immune responses, ease inflammatory discomfort, and strengthen everyday resilience.

Why It Works: Mechanism of Action

i-Anti-Inflammatory® 抗炎寶 aligns the gut–immune axis. Targeted probiotics, prebiotic fiber, and high-potency vitamins D3 + K2 work together to modulate immune signaling, reinforce the intestinal barrier, and support a calm, balanced inflammatory response.

Proudly Made in Hong Kong — Gut-Driven Immune Balance

Microbiome & Postbiotics

  • Precision probiotics help maintain a healthy microbial balance in the gut.
  • Prebiotic/dietary fiber feeds beneficial flora to generate short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) such as butyrate.[2]
  • SCFAs act as postbiotic signals that temper NF-κB activity and support mucosal repair and immune homeostasis.[1][2]

Barrier & Immune Modulation

  • Specific lactobacilli support tight-junction proteins (e.g., claudins/occludin) for a resilient epithelial barrier.[3]
  • Promotes balanced mucosal defenses (sIgA) and regulatory immune tone for coordinated—not overactive—responses.[5]
  • Helps maintain comfortable GI motility and day-to-day digestive comfort.

Vitamin D3 + K2 Synergy

  • Vitamin D3 supports innate defenses and healthy cytokine signaling; it helps keep immune activity appropriately regulated.[7]
  • Vitamin K2 complements D3 by activating vitamin-K-dependent proteins (e.g., MGP) relevant to vascular and calcium balance.[8]
  • Low sugar (0 g) and light energy (~20 kcal) make daily use easy.

Everyday Resilience

  • Designed to support recovery after daily stressors and activity.
  • Backs whole-body comfort via the gut–immune–metabolic network; probiotic use is associated with modest reductions in inflammatory markers (e.g., CRP) in RCT meta-analyses.[6]
