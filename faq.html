Some Positive Reactions
(These are for general information only)
好转反应（此数据仅供参考用途）

1. Acidic body condition 酸性体质者
Tiredness (daytime); thirst, frequent urination; gases and flatulence
爱困（在白天），喉干舌燥，频尿，放屁多

2. High blood pressure 高血压
Heavy headedness; dizziness in the first weeks
头会有重重的感觉，头晕现象持续一至两星期

3. Anemia 贫血者
Reactions may differ. May experience mild bleeding nose
反应因体质而异，但会有轻微流鼻血

4. Stomach problem 胃不好者
Heavy chest; heatiness; having no appetite
胸口发热的感觉；比较吃不下食物

5. Stomach ulcer 胃溃疡患者
Pain experience at site of ulceration; discomfort in stomach
溃疡部分会有疼痛感，肠内的感觉

6. "Drooping stomach" 胃下垂者
Uncomfortable feeling in stomach, nausea
胃部觉得不适，恶心感

7. Intestines problem 肠不好者
Diarrhea, conditions may differ
有腹泻的现象，依情况而异

8. Liver problem 肝不好者
Nausea; rashes or ulcers may appear on skin
恶心感；皮肤表面会有疹或溃疡出现

9. Liver cirrhosis 肝硬化者
May pass stools of blood or clot in stool
有时大便会带血块

10. Kidney problem 肾不好者
Protein in urine recorded; may feel weak and tired
小便带有蛋白质；有时会全身无力

11. Diabetic 糖尿病患者
May experience abnormal level of blood sugar; numb on arms and legs
血糖会有异常的现象；手脚会有麻痹感

Some Positive Reactions
(These are for general information only)
好转反应（此数据仅供参考用途）

12. Acne 青春痘者
May experience aggravation and then fade away
初期会稍微增加，但很快就会消失

13. Piles 痔疮者
May pass blood or blood clot in stool
排便时，有时会一时出现血丝或血块

14. Chronic bronchitis 慢性支气管炎
Thirst; nausea; dizziness; difficulty in clearing sputum
会有口干，呕吐，头昏，不易咳出痰等现象

15. Lung problems 肺不好者
More expectorant with cough of milky yellow color
咳嗽时，痰增多，带微黄色

16. Sinusitis 鼻窦炎者
May experience more and thicker discharge
有时会增加排鼻涕量，且呈现粘状

17. Sensitive skin 皮肤过敏者
Excretion of itchiness for few days and then problem subsides
初期皮肤较容易痒，几天后痒的现象便会消失

18. Cervical problem 子宫不好者
Increase menstrual discharge and pain; blood clot in discharge
初期月经量增加，或是变迟，或有血块

19. Low white blood count 白血球过少者
Thirst; many dreams; discomfort in stomach
渴望口干，梦多，胃不舒服

20. Muscle and nerve pain 肌肉、神经疼痛者
Depending on condition; pain may increase before going away
短期疼痛增加，症状改善便会消失

21. Rheumatisms 风湿痛者
Affected part may experience mild/severe pain within few days
患部会有轻微或严重疼痛，这反应会在数日内消失

22. Gout, post-natal cold 痛风者
Whole body lethargy and pain which subside after few days
全身会有乏力及疼痛的现象，这些会在数天后消失

23. High uric acid level 尿酸过高者
Ache all over and different reactions depending on severity
全身酸痛，视情况而有不同反应

24. Nervous system disorder 精神病患者
Unable to fall asleep; restlessness; depression
不但不能入睡，反而更清醒，情绪低落

Some users may experience mild and temporary reactions such as tiredness, thirst, changes in digestion (e.g., gas, stool, urination), or skin sensitivity. These are generally signs your body is adjusting and detoxifying. For example, those with high blood pressure may feel dizziness initially, while those with sensitive skin may notice slight itchiness that subsides. If symptoms persist, consult a healthcare provider.
