Enriched with key nutrients and targeted probiotics, it supports retinal protection, visual acuity, and relief from dry-eye discomfort associated with screen time and environmental stressors.

Why It Works: Mechanism of Action

i-Eyes® 明目寶 fortifies the retina–tear film–microbiome axis. Lutein esters build macular pigment to filter high-energy blue light and quench oxidative stress; vitamin D3 supports ocular surface immunity; vitamin K2 helps maintain healthy microvascular function; and probiotics nurture a balanced gut–eye axis for calmer ocular surfaces.

Proudly Made in Hong Kong — Vision Care for the Digital Era

Macular Pigment & Retinal Defense

  • Lutein esters supply lutein/zeaxanthin that accumulate in the macula as protective pigment, filtering blue light and acting as powerful antioxidants.[1–3]
  • Supplementation with lutein/zeaxanthin increases macular pigment and supports visual function, with large trials informing modern eye-health formulas.[2,4]

Tear Film & Ocular Surface Immunity

  • Vitamin D3 modulates innate/epithelial immunity and has been linked to improved symptoms and tear parameters in dry-eye populations.[5–7]
  • Targeted probiotics and their postbiotics can influence mucosal immunity and ocular-surface comfort via the gut–eye axis (microbiome–immune signaling).[8–9]

Microvascular & Vascular Wellness

  • Vitamin K2 activates vitamin-K-dependent proteins (e.g., MGP) involved in vascular integrity; supplementation has shown benefits for vascular-calcification endpoints in meta-analyses.[10]
  • Healthy microcirculation supports nutrient/oxygen delivery to ocular tissues.

Low-Burden, Daily-Use Formula

  • Balanced macros with added live probiotics (≥ 1.0 × 108 CFU) and essential vitamins for simple, everyday eye support.
  • No NRV is established for probiotics; effects are mediated through the microbiome and postbiotic signaling.[8–9]
