Why It Works: Mechanism of Action

i-Force® 動力寶 aligns the gut–muscle–immune axis. Targeted probiotics encourage short-chain fatty acid (SCFA) production, while vitamins D3 + K2 support immune tone and vascular elasticity—together helping sustain energy output and training consistency.

Proudly Made in Hong Kong — Microbiome-Powered Performance

Microbiome ➜ Muscle Energy

  • Probiotics help generate SCFAs (acetate, propionate, butyrate) that act as signalling fuels for skeletal muscle and mitochondria, supporting endurance capacity.[1]
  • Across RCTs, probiotic use shows small but meaningful gains in tests dominated by aerobic metabolism (e.g., time-to-exhaustion, VO2-related outputs).[2]

Gut Barrier & Recovery

  • Probiotics help maintain barrier integrity and moderate exercise-related GI/immune stress, which can reduce fatigue signals during prolonged efforts.[3]
  • Better gut comfort supports steady intake/hydration on training days, aiding performance consistency.

Immune Readiness

  • Probiotics reduce the number and duration of common upper-respiratory infections in the general population—helpful for keeping training on track.[4]
  • Vitamin D3 contributes to normal immune function—useful when training loads or seasons challenge resilience.[5]

D3 + K2 Vascular Support

  • Vitamin K2 (MK-7) activates matrix Gla-protein (MGP), supporting arterial elasticity; long-term MK-7 intake improved arterial stiffness in a randomized trial.[6]
  • Balanced D3 + K2 supports healthy calcium handling and vascular wellbeing—foundational for active lifestyles.[5][6]
