Probiotics & the Gut–Skin Axis

  • Balanced microbiota can reduce systemic inflammatory signals (e.g., IL-6, TNF-α) and improve epithelial barrier function, which translates to calmer, less reactive skin.
  • Clinical and meta-analytic data suggest specific probiotics help reduce eczema severity and support skin comfort in sensitive cohorts.

Sources: Salem et al., 2018, Int J Mol Sci — Gut–skin axis review; JAMA Dermatology 2018 — Probiotics & atopic dermatitis meta-analysis.

Vitamin D3 (+K2 MK-7) for Barrier & Immune Tone

  • Vitamin D3 supports epidermal differentiation, antimicrobial peptide expression, and immune modulation linked to improved barrier function and eczema control.
  • Vitamin K2 partners with D3 in healthy calcium handling and vascular support, aiding nutrient delivery to skin tissues.

Sources: Nutrients 2019 — Vitamin D supplementation & atopic dermatitis meta-analysis; Nutrients 2019 — Vitamin K2 MK-7 overview & extra-skeletal roles.

Zinc for Barrier Integrity & Repair

  • Zinc (15 mg) is essential for keratinocyte proliferation, tight-junction integrity, antioxidant defense (Cu/Zn-SOD), and normal wound repair—key elements for resilient, comfortable skin.

Source: Nutrients 2018 — The Role of Zinc in Wound Healing/Skin Health.
