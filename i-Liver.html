i-Liver® 護肝寶 — Liver Detox & Metabolic Support

i-Liver® is an advanced Probiotic Nutritional Drink (益生營養液飲料) that supports healthy liver function and internal cleansing. Its targeted probiotics and nutrients aid natural detoxification, help reduce hepatic fat accumulation, and promote metabolic clarity.

Why It Works: Mechanism of Action

i-Liver® 護肝寶 targets the gut–liver axis. Probiotics, prebiotic fiber, and bile-acid–active strains work together to reinforce the intestinal barrier, lower endotoxin load, and tune hepatic lipid handling for clearer metabolism and everyday liver comfort.

Proudly Made in Hong Kong — Gut–Liver Axis Support

Gut Barrier & Endotoxin Control

  • Lactobacillus strains upregulate tight-junction genes (claudin, occludin, ZO-1) to strengthen the epithelial barrier.[1][2]
  • Improved barrier function helps limit lipopolysaccharide (LPS) translocation to the portal vein, easing TLR4/NF-κB activation in the liver.[3][4]
  • Clinical evidence shows probiotics can modestly improve liver enzymes and steatosis in NAFLD populations.[6]

SCFAs, Bile Acids & Hepatic Metabolism

  • Prebiotic fermentation generates short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) that influence inflammation and lipid metabolism via FFAR2/3 signaling and epigenetic (HDAC) effects.[4]
  • Many Lactobacillus/Bifidobacterium possess bile salt hydrolase (BSH) activity, reshaping the bile-acid pool and engaging FXR/TGR5 pathways relevant to lipid and glucose homeostasis.[5]
  • Together these mechanisms support normal bile flow, cholesterol handling, and hepatic fat oxidation for a “lighter” metabolic state.[3][4][5]
