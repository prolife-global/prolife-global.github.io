i-Brain® 腦健寶 – Cognitive Vitality & Clarity

i-Brain® 腦健寶 is an advanced Probiotic Nutritional Drink (益生營養液飲料) that supports brain function and mental performance. Featuring neuro-supportive probiotics and targeted nutrients, it promotes focus, cognitive resilience, and healthy aging via the gut–brain axis.

Why It Works: Mechanism of Action

i-Brain® 腦健寶 supports the microbiota–gut–brain axis. Probiotics and prebiotic fiber encourage short-chain fatty acid (SCFA) production and neuromodulatory metabolites, while vitamins D3 + K2 help maintain healthy neuro-immune signaling and vascular flexibility—together supporting mental clarity and everyday cognitive performance.

Proudly Made in Hong Kong — Microbiome-Powered Brain Support

Microbiome → Neurotransmitters & Stress

  • Selective probiotics can influence GABA and stress circuitry via the vagus and HPA axis—mechanisms linked to calmer mood and better cognitive performance.[1][2]
  • In randomized trials, probiotic supplementation has shown benefits on aspects of cognition in older adults/MCI and reduced stress reactivity in healthy volunteers.[3][4]

SCFAs & Neuroinflammation

  • Prebiotic fiber is fermented to SCFAs (e.g., butyrate, acetate) that signal across the gut–brain axis and help tune microglial activity and neuroinflammatory tone.[5]
  • By supporting a robust mucosal barrier and balanced immunity, probiotics may contribute to clearer thinking under day-to-day stressors.[5]

Vitamin D3: Neuro-Immune Support

  • Vitamin D receptors are expressed in key brain regions; adequate D status supports normal neuro-immune function and brain homeostasis.[6]

Vitamin K2 (MK-7): Brain Lipids & Vascular Health

  • Vitamin K participates in sphingolipid metabolism and activates vitamin-K-dependent proteins relevant to neuronal survival and vascular elasticity—factors tied to healthy cognitive aging.[7]
