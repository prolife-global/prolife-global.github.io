Lactobacillus plantarum

  • Enhances gut microbiota diversity and suppresses fat-promoting bacteria (e.g., Firmicutes)
  • Boosts production of short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) like butyrate, which improve fat oxidation and reduce inflammation
  • Scientific Support: A 2024 meta-analysis of 9 RCTs showed significant reductions in BMI and abdominal fat with Lactobacillus plantarum supplementation 1

Source: 1. The Role of Lactobacillus plantarum in Reducing Obesity and Inflammation: A Meta-Analysis

Lactobacillus rhamnosus

  • Stimulates GLP-1 secretion to slow gastric emptying and enhance satiety
  • Reduces LPS levels, improving gut inflammation and insulin sensitivity
  • Scientific Support: Studies show L. rhamnosus GG improves GLP-1 secretion by protecting L-cells from lipotoxicity and modulating gut microbiota 2

Source: 2. The Role of Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG Supernatant Improves GLP-1 Secretion Through Attenuating L Cell Lipotoxicity and Modulating Gut Microbiota in Obesity
