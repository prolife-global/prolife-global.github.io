i-Hypertension® 降壓寶 is a Probiotic Nutritional Drink (益生營養液飲料) formulated to support circulatory health and stable blood pressure. A careful combination of probiotics and nutrients helps maintain vascular relaxation, heart rhythm regulation, and overall cardiovascular balance.

Why It Works: Mechanism of Action

i-Hypertension® 降壓寶 targets known levers of healthy blood-pressure control: microbiome-derived metabolites that support endothelial nitric-oxide signaling, electrolyte and vitamin cofactors for vascular tone, and nutrients linked with arterial elasticity.

Proudly Made in Hong Kong — Microbiome & Nutrient Synergy for Vascular Calm

Probiotics → SCFAs & Endothelial NO

  • Gut bacteria ferment fiber to short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) that signal through GPCRs and help modulate vascular tone and inflammation, supporting normal endothelial function.[1]
  • Meta-analyses of randomized trials report modest reductions in systolic/diastolic blood pressure with probiotic supplementation, particularly multi-strain and ≥8 weeks use.[2][3]

Magnesium & Smooth-Muscle Relaxation

  • Magnesium is a natural calcium antagonist that supports vascular smooth-muscle relaxation and endothelial function; clinical meta-analyses show small but significant BP improvements with supplementation.[4][5]

Vitamin D3 & Vascular/Autonomic Balance

  • Vitamin D receptors are present in vascular and cardiac tissues; adequate D status is associated with healthier endothelial and RAAS signaling, supporting normal pressure regulation.[6]

Vitamin K2 (MK-7) & Arterial Elasticity

  • Vitamin K activates matrix Gla-protein (MGP), a vascular vitamin-K-dependent protein that helps inhibit arterial calcification and supports arterial compliance—factors related to hemodynamic stability.[7][8]
