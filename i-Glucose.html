i-Glucose® 衝糖寶 – Metabolic Balance & Glucose Control

i-Glucose® 衝糖寶 is a Probiotic Nutritional Drink (益生營養液飲料) developed to help maintain healthy blood sugar and metabolic stability. Targeted probiotics and glucose-friendly nutrients support insulin sensitivity and steadier daily energy.

Why It Works: Mechanism of Action

i-Glucose® 衝糖寶 focuses on the microbiome–metabolism axis, pairing live probiotics with metabolic cofactors that assist insulin action and post-meal glucose handling.

Proudly Made in Hong Kong — Microbiome & Nutrient Synergy for Glycemic Balance

Probiotics → SCFAs, Incretins & Glucose

  • Gut microbes ferment fiber to short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) that activate FFAR2/FFAR3 and stimulate GLP-1 secretion, supporting insulin release and satiety.[1]
  • Meta-analyses of randomized trials show probiotic supplementation can modestly improve fasting glucose, HbA1c, and insulin resistance indices, especially with multi-strain formulas and ≥8 weeks use.[2][3]

Soluble Fiber → Lower Post-Meal Spikes

  • Viscous fibers slow gastric emptying and glucose absorption, reducing post-prandial glucose excursions and improving insulin demand.[4]

Magnesium → Insulin Signaling

  • Magnesium is required for insulin receptor phosphorylation and glucose transport; supplementation shows small but significant improvements in fasting glucose/insulin sensitivity in trials and meta-analyses.[5][6]

Vitamins D3 + K2 → Metabolic Support

  • Vitamin D receptors in pancreatic β-cells and muscle support insulin secretion and sensitivity; meta-analyses report modest benefits in deficient individuals.[7]
  • Higher dietary menaquinone (K2) intake has been linked to lower type-2 diabetes risk, and small RCTs suggest improved insulin sensitivity with vitamin K supplementation, potentially via carboxylation of VK-dependent proteins.[8][9]
